Privacy Policy

Geo Snap (GPS Map Camera)
Effective date: 10 November 2026
Contact: theindie67@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Geo Snap ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use Geo Snap (GPS Map Camera), including its camera, location-stamping, photo-management, PDF-export and premium features.

1. Information the app accesses

  • Camera: The app uses your device camera so you can take photographs.
  • Location: If you grant location permission, the app accesses your device location while you use the app to obtain location details that can be displayed on or associated with a photograph.
  • Photos and media: The app accesses or saves photographs and related files so you can view, manage, edit or export them.
  • Information you enter: If you use a feature that lets you edit the displayed location, the location text or other details you enter may be used to create the edited photo or document.

2. How photos and location information are stored

Based on the app's current design as described to us, photographs and their associated location details are stored locally on your device. Geo Snap does not require an account or login, and the app's core photo and location-stamping workflow is not intended to upload your photographs or GPS coordinates to a Geo Snap server.

When you choose to share a photograph or exported PDF using another app or service, that sharing is initiated by you. The receiving app or service will handle the information under its own privacy policy.

3. PDF export and premium features

Some features, such as editing displayed location information and creating PDF files, may be available only through a paid premium option. Information used to perform these features (for example, selected photographs, edited location text and document content) is processed to provide the feature. Based on the current information provided, these files are stored locally unless you choose to share them.

Premium access requires a purchase. The payment provider or app store shown during checkout processes the transaction. Geo Snap does not ask you to send payment-card details directly to us. We may receive purchase-related information such as whether a purchase is active so the app can unlock premium features. The exact information processed depends on the billing system used.

4. Advertising

Geo Snap uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interaction and diagnostic or advertising data to deliver, measure and help prevent fraud in advertising, subject to Google's policies and the settings available on your device.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Analytics and diagnostics

We are verifying whether any analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the current version of Geo Snap. If such services are used, they may collect app usage, device, performance or crash information to help us understand app reliability and improve the app. This section must be updated to identify the actual services and data collected before this policy is published.

6. When information is shared

We do not sell your photographs or GPS location information. Information may be processed by third-party services needed to operate features you use, such as advertising services and the payment provider for premium purchases. Information is also shared when you explicitly choose to export or share a photo or PDF through another app.

7. Data retention and deletion

Photos and exported files stored locally remain on your device until you delete them or remove them through your device's storage controls. Uninstalling the app may remove app-specific local data, but files saved to shared photo or document folders may remain and may need to be deleted separately. Advertising or purchase-related records held by third-party providers are subject to those providers' retention practices.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps in designing and maintaining the app to protect information handled by it. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for deciding where you share exported photographs and documents.

9. Children's privacy

Geo Snap is a general-purpose utility app and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a service we operate, contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this page with a revised effective date.
